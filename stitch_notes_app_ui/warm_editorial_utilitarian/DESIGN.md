## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes the quiet luxury of bespoke stationery with the ruthless precision of Swiss typography. Designed for reflective thinkers, writers, researchers, and knowledge architects, the experience rejects the chaotic gamification of standard productivity software in favor of an intentional, distraction-free sanctuary.

### Design Movement & Ethos
- **Editorial Warmth Meets Swiss Clarity:** Grounded by a quiet paper-textured canvas, generous typographic pacing, and sharp, structural gridlines. Layouts balance literary intimacy with architectural order.
- **Physicality Without Kitschy Skeuomorphism:** Subtle surface shifts, soft debossed borders, and warm stone surfaces invoke tactile paper stock, linen covers, and fountain-pen ink without mimicking literal page tears or drops.
- **Calm, High-Focus Ambiance:** Chrome recedes entirely. The interface operates as an invisible vessel, allowing personal essays, field notes, and synthetic research to claim primary hierarchy.

## Colors

The palette reproduces physical materialities: archival vellum, rich sumi ink, warm cognac leather, and dried botanical pigments.

### Roles & Semantic Mapping
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Light (`#FBFBFA`): Warm, unbleached stone vellum.
  - Surface Elevated Light (`#FFFFFF`): Crisp sheet white for individual cards, drawers, and floating panels.
  - Surface Recessed Light (`#F4F4F0`): Muted debossed foundation for callout blocks, code insets, and search tracks.
  - Surface Dark (`#18181B`): Deep charcoal-slate backing for ambient low-light reading.
- **Inks & Contrasts:**
  - Ink Heavy (`#191919`): Deep editorial body and headline tone; avoids harsh artificial pure black.
  - Ink Subdued (`#525250`): Supporting metadata, reading estimates, and secondary labels.
  - Ink Muted (`#8A8A85`): De-emphasized timestamps, inactive indicators, and breadcrumbs.
- **Accents:**
  - Cognac / Amber (`#C25E00`): Used strictly for focal interactions—curated bookmarks, primary actions, text selection highlights, and pinned status flags.
  - Forest Sage (`#3F6212`): Anchors taxonomy, organizational tags, metadata markers, and success signals.
- **Structural Outlines:**
  - Subtle Border (`#E5E5E3`): Precise hairlines separating zones without visual weight.
  - Dark Mode Border (`#27272A`): Balanced, low-luminance divider line.

## Typography

Typography is the foundational architecture of this design system. It establishes a deliberate dichotomy: Newsreader delivers literary cadence for prolonged contemplation and narrative flow, while Inter provides mechanical, functional utility for high-density toolbars, metadata, and structural navigation.

### Rules & Rhythm
- **Optical Pacing:** Longform notes mandate an expansive `1.65` to `1.75` line-height ratio on body sizes to mimic classical book layout and prevent eye strain.
- **Reading Measure:** Text columns should never exceed 68 characters per line (`ch`) in desktop reading views, centered gracefully on the page.
- **Italic Inflections:** Newsreader italics are reserved for citations, personal marginalia, timestamps within cards, and thematic subheadings.
- **Tabular Numerals:** Inter numerals in metadata, word counts, and search indices must utilize `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` to guarantee vertical baseline alignment across feeds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure marries dynamic Swiss module sizing with fluid multi-device adaptability. Everything aligns to an authoritative 4px sub-grid, with spacing variables derived from an 8px cadence.

### Device Fluidity & Adaptive Grid
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column dynamic stream. Full viewport width with `1.25rem` (20px) safety margins. Floating actions hover above safe areas with a 16px bottom offset.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 6-column fluid structure. Dual-pane view featuring a persistent 280px master note list alongside an expansive note editor canvas.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column adaptive layout. Three-tier navigation: slim icon sidebar (64px), folder/notebook panel (260px), and an offset note editor centered within an 8-column canvas with responsive margins.

### Vertical Spatial Hierarchy
- Tight metadata groupings (tag pills, date, word count) utilize `space-xs` and `space-sm`.
- Paragraph breaks within notes maintain a standard `space-md` bottom gap.
- Major document section separators utilize `space-xl` coupled with subtle 1px border dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering relies on tactile tonal planes and crisp hairlines rather than dramatic, muddy shadows. Elevation simulates layered physical cards cut from dense 300gsm paper stock.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat, unbordered `#FBFBFA` surface.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Surfaces):** Raised sheet white (`#FFFFFF`) framed with a razor-thin 1px solid border (`#E5E5E3`). Paired with a delicate, low-contrast shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.03), 0 4px 12px rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Drawers, Menus & Bottom Sheets):** Crisp white surface offset with a soft structural boundary: `0 -4px 24px rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Command & Capture Bar):** Floating pill suspended over content using a dual-layer shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(25, 25, 25, 0.04)`.
- **Level -1 (Recessed Well):** Inset code blocks and search rails utilize `#F4F4F0` with an inner border of `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.

## Shapes

The geometry balances the precision of straight book edges with soft, handheld ergonomics. The baseline shape token level is `2` (Rounded: 0.5rem base radius).

### Shape Rules by Functional Context
- **Card Enclosures & Modules (`rounded-md` - 8px):** Structural note cards, callout containers, and modal sheets use 8px corners to evoke cleanly guillotine-cut paper notebooks.
- **Pill Containers (`rounded-full` / `rounded-xl`):** Dynamic interactive elements—including the floating capture bar, tag badges, and quick-filter pills—use full radii (9999px) to establish tactile contrast against the rectangular grid.
- **Inner Nested Elements:** When a pill or button resides inside an 8px card, its radius scales down to 4px (`rounded-sm`) to preserve strict mathematical corner concentricity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#191919` surface with `#FFFFFF` text. On hover/active, transitions to `#C25E00`. Height: 40px (mobile 44px for thumb targets). 8px radius.
- **Secondary / Subdued:** Ghost outline with 1px `#E5E5E3`, text `#191919`. Active state shifts to a subtle `#F4F4F0` fill.
- **Icon Actions:** 36x36px clickable hit area with 20px centered monochrome stroke icons.

### Tactile Card Feeds
- **Structure:** `#FFFFFF` background encased in a 1px `#E5E5E3` stroke.
- **Content Flow:** Upper row displays category metadata in `label-caps` paired with an amber bookmark dot if pinned. Title is set in `headline-md` (Newsreader), followed by a 2-line snippet in `body-sans` (Inter) with `color: #525250`.
- **Footer:** Bottom edge features tag pills aligned left, and relative edit timestamps aligned right.

### Tag Pills
- **Palette & Sizing:** Height 22px, `rounded-full`, 8px horizontal padding.
- **Style:** Background tinted with 10% opacity Sage Olive (`rgba(63, 98, 18, 0.08)`) with text rendered in `#3F6212` using `label-md`.
- **Selected State:** Solid `#3F6212` background with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Modular Callout Blocks
- Left-accented structural callouts featuring a 3px solid vertical bar in `#C25E00`.
- Background set to recessed paper `#F4F4F0` with a 4px right-side radius.
- Internal typography set in `body-sans` with an italicized lead-in quote or insight anchor.

### Floating Quick-Capture Bar
- **Positioning:** Centered horizontally, anchored 24px above the bottom screen margin on mobile. Max-width 360px.
- **Surface:** `#FFFFFF` with the Level 3 elevation shadow and an ultra-fine 1px `#E5E5E3` perimeter.
- **Composition:** Left side houses a soft placeholder text ("Capture an idea..."), center holds icon tools (Voice memo, Camera OCR, Check-list), and the right terminus holds an amber-filled (`#C25E00`) circle trigger with a white "+" glyph.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Borderless or flat 1px bottom-line states inside active note views for uninterrupted typing.
- Global search uses a 44px recessed capsule (`#F4F4F0`), zero-border default, displaying a subtle magnifying glass icon and an inline `⌘K` keyboard chip right-aligned.

### Bottom Sheet Modals
- Top border features an 8px horizontal pill drag-handle in `#D4D4D0`.
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` with 16px top corner radii.
- Includes quick-tagging carousels, export format grids (Markdown, PDF, Plaintext), and notebook destination pickers.
